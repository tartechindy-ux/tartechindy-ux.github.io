Data Deletion Instructions

The "yt boi" app does not store personal data about Facebook users other than the Page owner's own access tokens.

If you have connected to the app and want any associated data removed:

  1. On Facebook, go to Settings & privacy → Settings → Apps and websites.
  2. Find yt boi and click Remove. This revokes the app's access.
  3. To request deletion of anything else, message Draconia Malfoid HP on Facebook with the subject "Data deletion request". We will delete it within 30 days and confirm.
